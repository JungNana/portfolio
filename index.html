About

I'm a UI/UX designer with over four years of experience in graphic design. As a freelancer I've worked on dozens of projects: from visual materials for social media and presentations to full-scale UI/UX design, including user flows, wireframes, competitor analysis and UI kit development.

I've worked with clients such as Fancy Pack, Art Gang Division, Dzhmili Foundation, Proxima Research International, Toxic Studio and others. My experience also includes PC game interfaces, advertising materials, video content for TikTok and Instagram, packaging and promotional products.

I combine my art education with modern UX research methods. Studying at Prjctr and Mobios School expanded my knowledge of web, mobile and product design and strengthened my ability to build logical, visually appealing and functional interfaces.
